About the creator

Hi, I’m Courtney.

I’m a speech-language pathologist who started making the apps I wished existed for my own sessions.

Working with children since 2014.
Providing virtual speech therapy since 2022.

Experience

I’ve worked with children ages 0–12 since 2014. My background spans home health and in-person school settings, supporting a broad range of communication profiles. That includes autism classrooms with speaking and non-speaking students, as well as life skills and functional skills classes.

Since 2022, I’ve worked as a virtual speech therapist, primarily with non-speaking students. Along the way, I’ve found so many ways to engage children by tapping into their special interests and using play-based tools that keep them motivated.

That work inspired me to create my own therapy materials, and now I’m excited to share them with you.

My approach

I’m laid-back and like to go with the flow. I prefer to have a variety of tools at my fingertips so I can follow a child’s interests, respond in the moment, and adapt each session to what they need.

Education

I hold a Master of Science in speech-language pathology and a bachelor’s degree in English. My love of English, linguistics, language, books, and literacy led to a passion for communication.

Frequently asked questions

Can I try a resource first?

The Telespeech Space TPT store includes free samples. Browse the store to see which activities have a sample available and what each sample includes.

What devices do the apps work on?

The featured apps are designed for computers and laptops. Check the requirements in each TPT listing before choosing a resource. A mobile-friendly website does not mean the activities themselves support phones or tablets.

Can I use these in person or in teletherapy?

The resources were created from Courtney’s experience in virtual and in-person therapy. Choose an activity that fits your students, goals, and session format, and check its TPT listing for specific features and requirements.

Where do I get the resources?

Every shop link on this page opens the Telespeech Space store on Teachers Pay Teachers. You can find full product details, available samples, and purchasing options there.
