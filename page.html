The SLP behind the space

Hi, I’m Courtney.

I’m a speech-language pathologist who has worked with children since 2014 and in teletherapy since 2022. Telespeech Space grew out of creating the resources I wanted for my own sessions.

My approach is simple: follow a child’s interests, stay flexible, and make room for play. These resources bring that spirit to your screen.

A few helpful details

Before you blast off.

A little more about finding the right resource for your sessions.

Can I try a resource first?

Yes! The Telespeech Space store includes free samples. Browse the collection on Teachers Pay Teachers to find an activity to try with your students.

What device will I need?

The featured interactive apps are designed for computers and laptops. Check each product listing for its full requirements and what’s included before choosing a resource.

Where do I get the resources?

All resources are available through the Telespeech Space store on Teachers Pay Teachers. Every shop link on this page takes you to the collection, where you can explore product details and download free samples or purchase resources.
